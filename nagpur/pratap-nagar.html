Industry Tech Training & Engineering Internships near Pratap Nagar, Nagpur

Pratap Nagar is one of Nagpur’s most vibrant student hubs, situated directly adjacent to VNIT Nagpur and Khamla. Due to its close 3.1 km distance to our Ramdaspeth facility, students can reach Skilleze in less than 7 minutes via Central Bazar Road or Bajaj Nagar. With hundreds of engineering hostellers and PG residents, Pratap Nagar is a prime feeder area for our Full Stack Python, MERN Stack, and Data Analytics cohorts.

Distance & Landmark

3.1 km from Pratap Nagar Square & Orange City Hospital to our Ramdaspeth center.

Commute Time

Average travel time is 7–9 mins, easily accessible via the Orange Line Metro corridor.

Center Access

Offline lab access at Flat no. 8A, Second Floor, Kanchana Kiran Apartment, behind Raymond Showroom, beside UCN Call Centre, Ramdaspeth, Nagpur.

Commute Guide

How to Reach Our Ramdaspeth Center from Pratap Nagar

Student-tested transit directions to reach our Ramdaspeth center without traffic delays.

Nagpur Metro Route

Quick 5-minute e-rickshaw or bike ride to Ramdaspeth via Central Bazar Road.

Aapli Bus & Public Transit

Frequent Aapli buses from Pratap Nagar Ring Road to Central Bazar Road and Ramdaspeth.

Two-Wheeler & Driving

Head east on Central Bazar Road through Khamla or Bajaj Nagar directly into Ramdaspeth.

Pro Tip:Drive via Bajaj Nagar Square and Congress Nagar to avoid the Ring Road traffic lights. You can easily reach our Ramdaspeth center in 7 minutes.
