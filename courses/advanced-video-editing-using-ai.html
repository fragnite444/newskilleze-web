Quick Answer

This is a 3 month hands-on course in video editing. You learn Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and the basics of After Effects, along with colour correction, sound clean-up, reels and shorts, and AI tools for captions, transcripts, background removal and upscaling. It suits students and small business owners who want to edit properly, and you finish with a portfolio of edited videos you can show to clients or employers.

Week-by-Week Syllabus — Advanced Video Editing Using AI

6 modules · 3 months (12 weeks · 96 hours of instruction)

Module 1Premiere Pro Foundations and Storytelling

Weeks 1-2
  • How a video project is organised: footage, folders, bins, sequences and saving safely
  • Importing clips, making a rough cut and trimming with the basic tools
  • Cuts, J-cuts and L-cuts: how to join two shots so the viewer does not notice
  • Story basics: a clear start, middle and end, even in a 30 second video
  • Pacing: when to cut fast, when to hold a shot, and how music beat helps
  • B-roll and cutaways: covering a bad shot or a long talking section
  • Keyboard shortcuts that make editing two or three times faster
Adobe Premiere ProAdobe Media EncoderGoogle Drive

Outcome: Edit a one-minute video from raw footage with clean cuts, sensible pacing and a clear story.

Module 2AI Captions, Transcript Editing and Sound Clean-up

Weeks 3-4
  • Text-based editing: turn speech into a transcript and cut the video by deleting words
  • Auto captions: generate, correct spelling mistakes and style them for Hindi, Marathi and English
  • Why AI captions need a human check, especially for mixed Hindi and English speech
  • Basic sound problems: low voice, fan noise, echo, and a voice that is too loud against music
  • Voice clean-up with AI: Enhance Speech and Voice Isolation, and where they sound fake
  • Mixing voice, music and sound effects at the right levels
  • Removing silences and filler words quickly, then checking by ear
Adobe Premiere ProDescriptAdobe PodcastDaVinci Resolve (Fairlight)

Outcome: Take a long talking video, cut it from the transcript, add correct captions and make the voice clear and even.

Module 3Colour Correction and Grading in DaVinci Resolve

Weeks 5-6
  • How Resolve is laid out: Media, Cut, Edit, Color and Deliver pages
  • Reading the scopes: simple ways to see if a shot is too dark, too bright or has a colour tint
  • Correcting first: exposure, white balance and skin tone so shots match each other
  • Grading second: giving the video a mood, such as warm wedding look or clean product look
  • Nodes, masks and tracking: fixing only a face, a sky or one product
  • Using LUTs properly, and why a LUT alone never fixes a badly shot clip
  • Matching shots from two different cameras or a phone and a camera
DaVinci ResolveAdobe Premiere Pro (Lumetri)

Outcome: Correct a set of mismatched clips so they look like one video, then add a simple, tasteful grade.

Module 4After Effects Basics, Background Removal and Upscaling

Weeks 7-8
  • After Effects basics: compositions, layers, keyframes and moving text
  • Simple motion graphics: animated titles, lower thirds with the speaker's name, and logo reveals
  • Green screen basics: keying out a green background and fixing rough edges
  • AI background removal without a green screen, and when it leaves edges that look wrong
  • Upscaling old or low quality footage with AI, and what it cannot save
  • Stabilising shaky phone videos and slowing down footage smoothly
  • Sending clips between Premiere Pro and After Effects without losing work
Adobe After EffectsRunwayTopaz Video AIDaVinci Resolve (Magic Mask)

Outcome: Add animated titles, remove or replace a background and improve a weak clip, while knowing where AI results need fixing.

Module 5Reels and Shorts Workflow

Weeks 9-10
  • Vertical video: 9:16 size, safe areas and keeping text clear of buttons and captions of the app
  • The first three seconds: writing a hook for a reel, a YouTube Short or a WhatsApp status
  • Cutting a long video into several short clips with AI help, then choosing the good ones yourself
  • Fast reel editing: beat-based cuts, zoom effects, transitions that do not look cheap
  • Captions for silent viewing: size, position and colour that are easy to read on a small phone
  • Batch workflow: editing a week of content for a shop or clinic in one sitting
  • Reading platform numbers like watch time to learn what to change in the next video
Adobe Premiere ProCapCutDescriptCanva

Outcome: Plan, edit and caption a set of reels for a local business in a repeatable way, in far less time per video.

Module 6Client Delivery, Export Settings and Portfolio

Weeks 11-12
  • Export settings explained: format, resolution, frame rate and bitrate in plain words
  • Right file for the right place: YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, a TV screen or a wedding hall projector
  • Working with clients: taking a brief, sending a draft, handling change requests and saying how many rounds are included
  • File handover: naming files, giving the project folder and keeping a backup
  • Capstone: edit a complete video for a real or practice client from raw footage to final delivery
  • Portfolio: a showreel of 60 to 90 seconds, a Drive or YouTube link and a short LinkedIn profile
  • Resume, mock interview and a practice client pitch with a mentor
Adobe Media EncoderAdobe Premiere ProDaVinci ResolveFrame.io

Outcome: Deliver a finished video in the right file formats, present your showreel and explain your work to a client or interviewer.

Frequently Asked Questions — Advanced Video Editing Using AI

Is video editing worth learning in 2026?
Yes, for most people. Almost every shop, clinic, coaching class and startup in Nagpur now posts videos, and many of them do not have anyone who can edit well. AI tools have made some tasks faster, but someone still has to decide what to cut, how long to hold a shot and whether the video tells its story. That part is the editor's job. We cannot promise a job or income, but the skill is useful both for employment and for freelance work.
What is the fee for this course?
Fees are shared by our advisor on a call or on WhatsApp. EMI-style payment options are available on enquiry. We do not put a fee on the website because it can depend on the batch and mode you choose.
Do I need any prior knowledge or editing experience?
No. You should be able to use a computer for basic work such as managing files and installing software. We begin with the first cut in Premiere Pro and go step by step. If you have edited on CapCut or a phone app, that helps a little, but it is not required.
Is the course offline in Nagpur or online?
Both. You can attend offline at our centre in Ramdaspeth, Nagpur, join live online on Zoom, or take a hybrid mode. The syllabus and the projects are the same in every mode. Batches are kept small so the teacher can look at your timeline and help you directly.
How is this different from learning on YouTube?
YouTube videos can teach you a tool, but they rarely tell you what is wrong with your own edit. Here a teacher watches your work and tells you where it drags or where the colour is off. You also get one-on-one mentor sessions, projects in a fixed order, and a portfolio at the end. The course covers everything in 12 weeks, starting with your first cut and ending with delivering a video to a client.
Can a student from commerce, arts or a non-IT background join?
Yes. Editing is more about eyes and ears than about coding or maths. Many people who learn it come from commerce, arts and science backgrounds. If you can use a laptop and are willing to practise, you can follow the course.
Do I have to buy Premiere Pro, After Effects and other software?
Please ask the advisor about software and system requirements when you enquire. DaVinci Resolve has a free version, and some tools in the course have free plans. A few AI features in Resolve and other tools need a paid plan, and we tell you clearly which ones in class so you can decide what you actually need.
Will AI replace video editors?
AI can now do some steps by itself, such as captions, removing silences and cleaning noise. But it makes mistakes with Hindi and Marathi words, leaves rough edges on cutouts and does not understand what a client wants. The course teaches you to use AI for the slow tasks and keep the creative decisions with you.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes. On completing the course you receive a Course Completion Certificate from Skilleze Technologies. The certificate shows you attended and finished the course. Your portfolio is what clients and employers will mostly look at.
