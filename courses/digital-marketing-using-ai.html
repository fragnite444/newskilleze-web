Quick Answer

This 12-week course teaches you how a local business gets found and gets enquiries online. You learn SEO, Google Business Profile, Instagram and Facebook, content calendars, email and WhatsApp marketing, Google Analytics 4 and a first look at paid ads. AI tools help you with drafts and ideas, but you do the thinking and the checking. You build a real marketing plan and a monthly report for one business.

Week-by-Week Syllabus — Digital Marketing Using AI

6 modules · 3 months (12 weeks · 96 hours of instruction)

Module 1Marketing Basics and Using AI the Right Way

Weeks 1-2
  • What digital marketing means: how a customer finds, trusts and contacts a business online
  • Know the customer: who buys from a Nagpur clothes shop, a dental clinic or a coaching class, and what they search for
  • Offer, channel and goal: writing a simple one-page marketing plan
  • Checking competitors: looking at what nearby businesses post and say
  • Using ChatGPT or Gemini as a helper: how to ask clearly, how to check facts, how to rewrite in your own words
  • What you should never paste into an AI tool, such as customer phone numbers or GST details
  • Keeping a simple work tracker in Google Sheets
ChatGPTGeminiGoogle Sheets

Outcome: Write a simple marketing plan for a local business and use an AI tool to help with drafts while checking every fact yourself.

Module 2SEO and Google Business Profile

Weeks 3-4
  • How Google search works, in simple words: crawling, ranking and why some shops show first
  • Keyword research: what people type, like dentist near me or best biryani in Dharampeth
  • Using Google Trends and Google Keyword Planner to see what people search for
  • On-page SEO: page title, headings, photos, page speed and mobile view
  • Setting up Google Business Profile: category, address, hours, photos, products and posts
  • Reviews: how to ask for them politely and how to reply to a bad one
  • Google Search Console basics: seeing which searches bring people to a website
Google Business ProfileGoogle Keyword PlannerGoogle Search ConsoleGoogle Trends

Outcome: Set up and improve a Google Business Profile for a local shop and choose the right search words for its website.

Module 3Social Media and Content Calendars

Weeks 5-6
  • Picking the right platform for a business: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or LinkedIn
  • Setting up a business profile: name, bio, highlights, link and contact button
  • Content ideas: offers, behind the scenes, customer stories, tips, and short reels
  • Making a 30-day content calendar in Google Sheets with three or four content themes
  • Designing posts in Canva with a brand kit so every post looks like the same business
  • Using AI for caption ideas and hashtags, then editing them so they sound like a real person
  • Scheduling posts and replying to comments and messages
InstagramMeta Business SuiteCanvaChatGPT

Outcome: Plan and schedule a month of posts for a business, with designs made in Canva and captions written by you.

Module 4Email and WhatsApp Marketing

Weeks 7-8
  • Why you must ask permission before messaging anyone, and what India's data protection law (the DPDP Act) means for a small business
  • Collecting customer contacts honestly: sign-up forms, QR codes and enquiry forms
  • WhatsApp Business app: business profile, catalogue, labels, quick replies and away messages
  • Broadcast lists and click-to-chat links, and how to avoid being reported as spam
  • Email basics: building a list, writing a subject line people open, and keeping emails short
  • Making a simple email template and sending a test before the real send
  • Writing a welcome message and a festival offer message, with AI giving a first draft and you fixing it
WhatsApp Business appMailchimpCanva

Outcome: Set up a WhatsApp Business account with a catalogue and send a proper permission-based email and WhatsApp offer.

Module 5Analytics, Basic Ads and Reporting

Weeks 9-10
  • Setting up Google Analytics 4 on a website and checking that it works
  • Reading GA4 in plain words: users, sessions, traffic sources, engagement and conversions
  • UTM links: tagging your links so you know which post or message brought the visitor
  • Intro to paid ads: boosting a post, the Meta Ads Manager screen and a simple Google search ad
  • Thinking about a small budget, and why you test with a little money first
  • Making a monthly report in Looker Studio or Google Sheets that a shop owner can read in five minutes
  • Learning from the numbers: what to do more of and what to stop
Google Analytics 4Looker StudioMeta Ads ManagerGoogle Ads

Outcome: Read basic website numbers, tag your links, and explain a month of results to a business owner in simple words.

Module 6Capstone Marketing Plan and Career Prep

Weeks 11-12
  • Choosing one real or practice business and writing its three-month marketing plan
  • Running two weeks of content, Google Business Profile updates and WhatsApp messages
  • Preparing the final report with numbers, screenshots and what you would do next
  • Presenting the plan to your batch and your mentor, and taking feedback
  • Resume and LinkedIn profile for a digital marketing role, with your work shown as case studies
  • Mock client pitch: explaining your plan to a shop owner who has never done online marketing
  • Mock interview and a final review with your mentor
Google SheetsCanvaLooker StudioLinkedIn

Outcome: Present a full marketing plan and report for one business, and explain your work in an interview or a client meeting.

Frequently Asked Questions — Digital Marketing Using AI

Is digital marketing worth learning in 2026?
Yes, if you are ready to practise. Most customers search on Google or check Instagram before they visit a shop, so businesses need people who can handle this. AI makes the first draft faster, but a person still has to know the customer and check the work. We cannot promise a job or an income, and results depend on your effort and the business you work with.
What is the fee for the Digital Marketing Using AI course?
Fees are shared by our advisor on a call or on WhatsApp. EMI-style options are available on enquiry. This way you get the correct details for the batch you want.
Do I need any prior knowledge?
No. We start from what digital marketing means and build step by step. If you can use a phone and a browser, you can join. Every tool is explained when it first comes up.
Is the course offline in Nagpur or online?
You can pick offline at our Ramdaspeth centre in Nagpur, online live on Zoom, or hybrid. Batches are small, and 1-on-1 mentor sessions are part of the course in every mode.
How is this different from YouTube videos?
YouTube videos teach one tool at a time and often skip the thinking behind it. Here you follow a plan, work on a real business case, and get feedback from a teacher and a mentor. You also learn how to check what AI tools write, which videos rarely cover.
I am not from an IT background. Can I join?
Yes. Students from commerce, arts, science and other streams can join. There is no coding. You need to be curious about people and how they decide what to buy.
Will AI do my work for me in this course?
No. You use AI tools for drafts, ideas and quick checks. You choose the idea, fix the facts, and write in a way that sounds like the business. A client can tell when a post was copied straight from an AI tool, and so can customers.
Does the course teach paid ads in detail?
It gives you a first look at Meta Ads and Google Ads so you understand the basics. If you want to run ad campaigns properly, our Performance Marketing Using AI course goes much deeper into targeting, tracking and budgets.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes. You get a Course Completion Certificate — Skilleze Technologies. Employers and clients will also want to see your plan, your report and your case studies, so we help you prepare those.
