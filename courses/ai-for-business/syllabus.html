Module-by-Module Curriculum

Module 1:What AI Can and Cannot Do for a Small Business

Weeks 1-2

Core Topics

  • How a chatbot like ChatGPT works, in plain words: it guesses the next word, it does not know your shop
  • Why AI sometimes gives a wrong answer in a confident voice, and how to catch it
  • ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini: what each is good at, and the difference between free and paid plans
  • Setting up your accounts and trying your first ten useful requests
  • Listing the work in your business that eats time: messages, quotations, posts, reports
  • Picking three small tasks to try first and noting how long they take today
  • First safety rules: what you should never paste into an AI tool, like bank details or customer lists

Tools & Tech

ChatGPTClaudeGemini

Learning Outcomes

  • Explain in simple words what AI tools can and cannot do, and pick three tasks in your own business to try first.

Module 2:Writing Clear Instructions for Business Work

Weeks 3-4

Core Topics

  • What a prompt is: the message you type to the AI
  • What to tell it: who you are, what you want, who it is for and how the answer should look
  • Giving your business details once and reusing them: shop name, products, tone, city
  • Asking in English, Hindi, Marathi or Hinglish, and checking that the result sounds natural
  • Asking for a short answer, a table, a list or a message that fits on WhatsApp
  • Fixing a poor answer by giving feedback instead of starting again
  • Saving your best prompts in a Notion page so your staff can use them too

Tools & Tech

ChatGPTClaudeNotion

Learning Outcomes

  • Write prompts that give usable answers for your business, and keep a saved list that your staff can copy and use.

Module 3:Sales Follow-ups and Customer Messages

Weeks 5-6

Core Topics

  • Keeping leads in a Google Sheet: name, phone number, what they asked for, last contact and next step
  • Writing a follow-up for a customer who asked for the price and then went silent
  • Quotation and proposal wording for a workshop job, a dealer order or an interior project
  • Replies to common answers like 'price is high' or 'I will tell you later'
  • WhatsApp Business basics: labels, quick replies, greeting message and product catalogue
  • Teaching the AI your own way of writing so messages do not sound like a machine
  • Why you read and edit every message before you press send

Tools & Tech

ChatGPTGoogle SheetsWhatsApp Business

Learning Outcomes

  • Run a weekly follow-up routine for your leads, with AI drafting the messages and you checking them.

Module 4:Content for Instagram, Facebook and Google Business Profile

Weeks 7-8

Core Topics

  • Post and reel ideas for your kind of business, such as a cloud kitchen, a boutique or a coaching class
  • Writing captions, hashtags and short reel scripts in your own voice
  • Making posters, offer banners and festival greetings in Canva
  • Using Canva's AI tools for text and image edits, and spotting where the result looks fake
  • Product descriptions for your catalogue and website
  • Google Business Profile: posting updates and replying to reviews politely
  • Planning a 30 day content calendar in a Google Sheet

Tools & Tech

CanvaChatGPTGoogle Business Profile

Learning Outcomes

  • Plan and make a month of posts for your business, with captions and designs ready to publish.

Module 5:Customer Support with AI

Weeks 9-10

Core Topics

  • Collecting the 30 questions customers ask most: delivery time, price, return, warranty, payment
  • Writing a clear FAQ document that the AI can use to draft answers
  • Drafting replies for complaints, delays and refunds that stay polite and firm
  • WhatsApp Business quick replies, away message and business hours
  • Where a simple chatbot helps and where a person must answer
  • Handling angry or sensitive messages: when you step in yourself
  • Checking every draft against your real policy before it goes out

Tools & Tech

ClaudeChatGPTWhatsApp Business

Learning Outcomes

  • Set up a FAQ and a reply pack so you or your staff can answer common customer messages quickly and in the same polite way.

Module 6:Bookkeeping Help and Monthly Reports

Weeks 11-14

Core Topics

  • What AI can help with (sorting expenses, reading a bill, explaining a sheet) and what it cannot do (be your CA)
  • Cleaning a messy sales or expense sheet taken from your billing software or bank statement
  • Putting expenses into groups like rent, salary, raw material and transport
  • Checking GST bills for missing details like GSTIN or date, using a checklist from your own CA
  • Formulas, filters and pivot tables in Google Sheets with AI help, and how to check the totals yourself
  • Asking Gemini or ChatGPT which product sells best and which month was weak
  • Making a one-page monthly report for yourself or your partner, while tax and filing decisions stay with your CA

Tools & Tech

Google SheetsGeminiChatGPT

Learning Outcomes

  • Prepare a clean monthly sales and expense sheet with a short report, and know which parts you still hand to your CA.

Module 7:Hiring and Managing Your Team

Weeks 15-16

Core Topics

  • Writing a job post for a shop assistant, delivery rider or sales executive
  • Interview questions for each role and a simple scoring sheet
  • Reading CVs with AI help, and why AI should never reject a person on its own
  • Drafts for offer letters and a joining checklist, which you review before use
  • Work instructions for daily tasks: how to pack an order, how to open and close the shop
  • Training notes and short quizzes for new staff in simple language
  • Meeting notes and task lists in Notion

Tools & Tech

ChatGPTNotionGoogle Docs

Learning Outcomes

  • Write a job post, an interview plan and two work instructions for your own team.

Module 8:Simple Automations with Zapier or Make

Weeks 17-19

Core Topics

  • What an automation is: when one thing happens, another thing is done without you
  • What you can do on the free plans, and where the limits start
  • A Google Form for enquiries that adds a row in a Google Sheet and sends you an email
  • An alert for each new lead and a reminder on the day a follow-up is due
  • Letting AI sort or summarise each enquiry before it reaches you
  • Testing, fixing errors and switching an automation off when needed
  • Keeping a person in the loop: at first, no message reaches a customer without your check

Tools & Tech

ZapierMakeGoogle FormsGoogle Sheets

Learning Outcomes

  • Build and test one automation that saves you a repeated task, and explain how to switch it off if it goes wrong.

Module 9:Tools Worth Paying For and Data Privacy Basics

Weeks 20-21

Core Topics

  • Testing a tool before you pay: one week, one real task, and a count of time saved
  • Comparing the paid plans of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini by what you actually use
  • Working out the monthly cost in rupees, how the payment is made and how to cancel
  • What happens to the text you type: privacy settings, and the option to stop your chats being used for training where the tool offers it
  • Rules for staff: what can and cannot be pasted, and using separate work accounts
  • Customer data and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 in plain words: ask permission, collect only what you need, keep it safe. This is basic awareness, not legal advice
  • Writing a one-page AI use rule sheet for your business

Tools & Tech

ChatGPTClaudeGemini

Learning Outcomes

  • Decide which AI tools are worth paying for in your business, and write a one-page rule sheet on safe use for your staff.

Weeks 22-24

  • A one-page plan: tools, monthly cost, who does what, and safety rules
  • Presenting your plan and a live demo to your batch and mentor
  • Training one staff member or family partner to use your prompts and sheets
  • For those looking for work or clients: resume, LinkedIn profile, portfolio link, and a mock interview or client pitch
