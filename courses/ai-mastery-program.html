Quick Answer

The AI Mastery Program is a 4-month practical course on using AI tools in daily study and work. You learn how these tools work, how to write good prompts, and how to use AI for writing, images, audio, video, spreadsheets and simple automations. You also build a small AI assistant without heavy coding, and you finish with a project you can show.

Week-by-Week Syllabus — AI Mastery Program

8 modules · 4 months (16 weeks · 128 hours of instruction)

Module 1How AI Tools Work, in Plain Words

Weeks 1-2
  • What AI, machine learning and a chatbot mean, using daily examples like UPI fraud alerts and YouTube suggestions
  • How a chatbot guesses the next word, and why it can sound sure even when it is wrong
  • The main tools: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity, and what each one is good at
  • Free plan and paid plan: what changes, and how to decide before you pay for anything
  • Tokens and the context window in simple words: why a long chat starts to forget
  • Setting up accounts, saving chats and keeping your private data safe
  • First task: write a polite reply to an angry customer message in three tools and compare the results
ChatGPTGoogle GeminiClaudePerplexity

Outcome: Explain in simple words how a chatbot works, pick a suitable tool for a task, and know what you should never paste into it.

Module 2Prompting That Gets Useful Answers

Weeks 3-4
  • A prompt is only an instruction: who is asking, what you need, for whom, and in what format
  • Giving context and examples so the answer fits your shop, class or office
  • Asking for tables, lists, short WhatsApp messages and step-by-step plans
  • Follow-up prompts: how to fix a weak answer instead of starting again
  • Writing prompts in English, Hindi or Marathi, and changing the tone of the reply
  • Saving your best prompts in one notes page so you can reuse them
  • Practice: product descriptions for a Nagpur clothes shop and a weekly menu post for a cloud kitchen
ChatGPTGoogle GeminiClaudeGoogle Docs

Outcome: Write clear prompts, fix weak answers with follow-up questions, and keep a small set of prompts that work for your own tasks.

Module 3Text, Image, Audio and Video Tools

Weeks 5-6
  • Making posters and Instagram post designs in Canva, including its AI features
  • Making images with Adobe Firefly and with the image options inside ChatGPT and Gemini
  • What image tools still do badly: spelling on posters, hands, logos and real product details
  • Writing a short reel script with AI and turning it into an edited video in CapCut
  • Turning a script into a voiceover with ElevenLabs, and why you need permission before copying anyone's voice
  • Adding captions and subtitles to reels, and fixing the mistakes in them
  • Checking every AI image and video for errors, and for copyright problems, before you post
CanvaAdobe FireflyElevenLabsCapCut

Outcome: Make a simple poster, a short reel with voiceover and captions, and a set of images for a small business, and check them before posting.

Module 4Research, Reading and Writing with AI

Weeks 7-8
  • Searching with Perplexity and opening the sources it shows instead of trusting the summary
  • Reading a long PDF, like a college syllabus or a GST notice, and asking questions about it in NotebookLM
  • Making summaries, short notes and flashcards for exam study
  • Drafting emails, letters, job applications and reports, then editing them until they sound like you
  • Fact-checking: finding wrong numbers, wrong names and made-up references
  • Translating and proofreading between English, Hindi and Marathi, and why you must read the result
  • Making a short slide deck from your own notes
PerplexityNotebookLMChatGPTGoogle Docs

Outcome: Research a topic with sources you have opened yourself, summarise a long document, and write a clean draft that you have checked line by line.

Module 5Spreadsheets and Simple Data with AI

Weeks 9-10
  • Asking AI to explain a formula you do not understand
  • Getting formulas for totals, filters, lookups and dates in Google Sheets and Excel
  • Cleaning messy data: repeated names, wrong date formats, phone numbers with extra spaces
  • Building a monthly sales sheet for a shop and a fee tracker for a coaching class
  • Asking questions about your data and making simple charts
  • Checking the AI's answer with a small manual calculation, for example a GST total
  • Why you should not upload customer, patient or student data to a public AI tool
Google SheetsMicrosoft ExcelChatGPT

Outcome: Build and clean a working sheet with AI help, and always check the numbers yourself before sharing them.

Module 6Simple Automations Without Code

Weeks 11-12
  • What automation means: when one thing happens, the next step runs by itself
  • Triggers and actions with a daily example: a new Google Form enquiry is saved in a sheet and an email is sent
  • Building flows in Zapier and in Make, and how they differ
  • Adding an AI step to a flow: sort enquiries, draft a reply, shorten a long message
  • Sending yourself a daily summary of new orders or enquiries
  • Testing a flow, reading error messages and fixing what broke
  • Free plan limits, and which tasks are better left to a person
ZapierMakeGoogle FormsGoogle Sheets

Outcome: Build a small automation that saves enquiries and drafts replies, test it with sample data, and explain what it can and cannot do.

Module 7Build Your Own AI Assistant and Use AI Responsibly

Weeks 13-14
  • What a custom assistant is: a chatbot with fixed instructions and your own documents
  • Writing the instruction page: role, tone, language and what the assistant must refuse to do
  • Building one with Custom GPTs in ChatGPT, Gemini Gems or Claude Projects
  • Adding your own document, such as a price list, an FAQ sheet or a fee structure
  • Which of these need a paid plan, and what to do if you only have a free account
  • Testing the assistant with tricky questions and fixing its wrong answers
  • Privacy, bias, copyright, and being honest when you used AI in college or office work; spotting fake images and cloned voices
ChatGPT (Custom GPTs)Google Gemini (Gems)Claude (Projects)

Outcome: Build a small assistant for a real business or class, test it properly, and follow clear rules on privacy and honest use.

Module 8Capstone Project and Career Preparation

Weeks 15-16
  • Choose a real problem: a shop, a coaching class, a clinic, a college club or your own office
  • Plan the project: which steps AI will do and which steps you will do yourself
  • Build and test it, with 1-on-1 mentor reviews along the way
  • Write a short project note: the problem, what you built, what went wrong and what you fixed
  • Resume: how to describe your AI skills honestly, with examples a recruiter can check
  • LinkedIn profile and a simple portfolio link on Notion or Google Drive
  • Mock interview and a short project presentation
NotionCanvaLinkedIn

Outcome: Present a finished project, show it through a portfolio link, and answer common interview questions about your AI work.

Frequently Asked Questions — AI Mastery Program

Is the AI Mastery Program worth learning in 2026?
AI tools are now part of normal study, office work and small business work, and they change every few months. A course helps you learn the basics that stay useful even when a tool changes. It will not do the work for you, and no course can promise a job or income. What you get is practice, feedback and projects you can show.
What is the fee for the AI Mastery Program?
Fees are shared by an advisor on a call or on WhatsApp. EMI-style payment options are available on enquiry. Please ask for the current details before you decide.
Do I need prior knowledge or coding skills?
No. You should be able to use a laptop, a browser and WhatsApp. We start with how these tools work in plain words, and every module adds one skill at a time. The automation and assistant modules use visual tools, so you do not write code.
Is the course offline in Nagpur or online?
Both. You can attend offline at our Ramdaspeth centre in Nagpur, online live on Zoom, or in a hybrid mode that mixes the two. Batches are kept small, and 1-on-1 mentor sessions are part of the course. The load is about 8 hours a week.
How is this different from learning on YouTube?
YouTube videos are free and useful for single tricks. In class you work on a real task, get your mistakes pointed out the same day, and finish three projects in a fixed order. A mentor reviews your work one on one. Tools also change fast, and a teacher can tell you what has changed.
Can a non-IT, commerce or arts student join?
Yes. Most of the course is about using tools on everyday work like writing, posters, sheets and customer messages. A commerce student can use it for accounts and GST sheets. An arts student can use it for research, writing and content. No technical background is expected.
Do I have to pay for AI tools during the course?
Many tools have a free plan, and we use those wherever possible. Some features, like building your own custom assistant, may need a paid plan from the tool company. Plans and limits change often, so your advisor will tell you what is needed before you start.
Will this course make me an AI engineer?
No. This is a course for using AI tools well, not for building AI models or writing machine learning code. If you later want that direction, a Python-based course like Generative AI is the better next step. This course gives you a base and a clear idea of what you like.
Do you guarantee a job after the course?
No. We do not promise jobs or income. The course prepares you with skills, projects, a resume and a mock interview, and you can apply for roles like content, social media, support and operations. The result depends on your effort and on what employers need at that time.
