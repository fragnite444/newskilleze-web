Module-by-Module Curriculum

Module 1:Paid Ads Basics and Money Maths

Weeks 1-2

Core Topics

  • How paid ads differ from posts and SEO, and where a paid ad fits in a business
  • The customer path in plain words: sees the ad, clicks, enquires, buys
  • Key terms explained simply: impressions, reach, CTR, CPC and CPM
  • CPL, CPA and ROAS with rupee examples: if you spend ₹5,000 and get 25 enquiries, what does each one cost
  • Profit and break-even: why a cheap lead is no use if the shop makes no profit on the sale
  • Setting up a Meta Business account and a Google Ads account the right way
  • How practice works in this course: small real budgets or sandbox accounts, and why results differ for every business

Tools & Tech

Google SheetsMeta Business SuiteGoogle Ads

Learning Outcomes

  • Explain CPL, CPA and ROAS to a shop owner with a worked rupee example and decide if a campaign is worth running.

Module 2:Audience, Keyword Research and Landing Pages

Weeks 3-4

Core Topics

  • Writing a simple customer profile for a business: age, area, need, and when they buy
  • Meta audiences: location radius around a Nagpur shop, age, interests and customer lists
  • Google keyword research: search words, match types and negative keywords that stop wasted clicks
  • Looking at competitor ads in the Meta Ad Library
  • What a good landing page has: one goal, a clear headline, an offer, a short form and a WhatsApp button
  • Why mobile speed matters, since most people open ads on a phone
  • Building and publishing a simple landing page for a practice business

Tools & Tech

Google Keyword PlannerMeta Ad LibraryWixWordPress

Learning Outcomes

  • Define a target audience, make a keyword list, and publish a landing page that has one clear action.

Module 3:Meta Ads for Facebook and Instagram

Weeks 5-6

Core Topics

  • Campaign, ad set and ad: how the account is arranged and what each level controls
  • Choosing an objective: Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads or Sales
  • Budget: daily and lifetime, and how to start small
  • Lead forms and click-to-WhatsApp ads for local business
  • Ad creatives: image, carousel and reel, and what makes people stop scrolling
  • Placements, ad review and why some ads get rejected
  • Launching a small practice campaign and reading the first results after a few days

Tools & Tech

Meta Ads ManagerCanvaMeta Ad Library

Learning Outcomes

  • Set up and launch a small Meta lead campaign with a clear audience, budget and creative.

Module 4:Google Ads: Search, Display and YouTube

Weeks 7-8

Core Topics

  • Google Ads structure: campaigns, ad groups, keywords and ads
  • Search campaigns: writing responsive search ads, adding call and location details
  • Quality Score and ad rank, explained in simple words
  • Bidding options in plain terms: manual clicks and automatic bidding, and when each is sensible
  • Display ads: images, audiences and why they work differently from Search
  • YouTube ads: short video ads, who sees them and what to measure
  • A short introduction to Performance Max, which is shown but not practised in depth

Tools & Tech

Google AdsGoogle Keyword PlannerYouTube Studio

Learning Outcomes

  • Build a Search campaign for a Nagpur business and explain when Display or YouTube ads make sense.

Module 5:Tracking, A/B Testing and AI for Ads

Weeks 9-10

Core Topics

  • Why tracking matters: without it you only guess which ad brought the enquiry
  • Installing the Meta Pixel and Google Tag Manager, and checking that they fire
  • Conversion tracking in Google Ads and GA4 for form fills, calls and WhatsApp clicks
  • A/B testing: change one thing at a time, wait for enough data, and know when to stop
  • Using AI tools for ad copy variations and image ideas, then checking them against the ad rules
  • Not promising what the business cannot deliver, even if an AI tool writes a strong line
  • Looking at how people behave on a page using heatmaps and recordings

Tools & Tech

Google Tag ManagerMeta PixelGoogle Analytics 4ChatGPT

Learning Outcomes

  • Set up working conversion tracking, run a simple A/B test, and use AI for copy ideas without breaking ad rules.

Module 6:Reporting, Capstone and Career Prep

Weeks 11-12

Core Topics

  • Building a report in Looker Studio or Google Sheets that shows spend, leads, cost per lead and what to change
  • Explaining results to a business owner in plain words, including when results are poor
  • What to check when an ad does not work: tracking, offer, landing page, audience, then budget
  • Handling client ad accounts safely: partner access, never sharing passwords, and keeping payment details private
  • Capstone: finishing your campaign work and final report for one business
  • Resume and LinkedIn profile that show your campaigns as case studies
  • Mock client call and mock interview with your mentor

Tools & Tech

Looker StudioGoogle SheetsLinkedIn

Learning Outcomes

  • Deliver a clear campaign report and explain it to a client or an interviewer, including what went wrong and what you would change.
